A robot that hangs from 4 cables attached in the corners of a room. It is capable of pulling on those cables with motors to move around the room.

# Project Overview
Using 4 of these motors https://a.co/d/05V1zQCu, we will 3D print pulleys to winch in paracord to lift a 3D printed body containing a Raspberry Pi 4 and an Arduino Uno. The bottom will have a hole pattern for accessory projects.
Ex. Arm, Cameras/Sensors, etc.
